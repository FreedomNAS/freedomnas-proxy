Caddy stops and stays off at boot; the apps behind it go back to their own ports, over plain HTTP.
Set up the reverse proxy

Caddy is installed from your edition's own packages if it is not there yet, and FreedomNAS takes over its configuration (the one it came with is kept beside it). The apps of the Containers catalogue then come in behind it by themselves; other services can be added below.

Your own domain

With a domain of your own, every site also answers as https://jellyfin.<your domain>/ with a Let's Encrypt certificate, which every device trusts with nothing to install. The certificate comes through your DNS provider (the DNS-01 challenge): no port of the NAS is opened to the internet. It is renewed by itself.

One KEY=value per line, with the names lego gives them for your provider (lego's list of DNS providers). They are kept on the NAS for root only; leave them empty to keep the saved ones.
Sites

Each site answers over HTTPS on its port of the NAS and passes the requests to the service behind it. Install FreedomNAS's certificate authority once on each device (System → Certificate) and none of them warns.
